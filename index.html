Adventures in Fire Island

Here are some pictures and memories from my trip to Fire Island.

I had a nice time at Fire Island. I walked along the trails, looked at the lighthouse, and saw a deer near the dunes. I also watched the sun set. My favorite part was being outside and enjoying the quiet views.
